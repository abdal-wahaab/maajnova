Our story

Technology tailored for you.

Maajnova started from a simple frustration: African businesses were being handed software built for somewhere else.

Tools that assumed fast internet, foreign payment methods and workflows that had nothing to do with how things actually work here. When it broke — and it always broke — there was no one in the room to fix it. So we built a studio to do it properly — one senior team, from first conversation to launch and beyond. No handoffs, no juniors learning on your budget, no black boxes.

So today we're building custom software, AI automation and business & healthcare systems for African businesses — starting with our own flagship product, Maaj Health, a hospital management platform built for African facilities.

Every project is personal. Every detail is considered.

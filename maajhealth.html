The problem

One platform for the whole facility.

Most hospitals and clinics still run on a patchwork of paper files, spreadsheets and disconnected tools — so records get lost, billing leaks and stock runs out.

Maaj Health is built to bring the whole facility into one secure system — front desk, wards, pharmacy, finance and management, all working from the same live information. White-label, so each hospital can run it under its own name, and designed for African healthcare from the ground up.

What's inside

Everything a facility runs on, in one system.

From patient records to pharmacy, Maaj Health brings your entire operation together — so you can focus on what matters most: better care.

Patient Records (EMR)

Billing & Claims
